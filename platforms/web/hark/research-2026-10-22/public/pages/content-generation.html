Content Generation and Publication Policy

This Content Generation and Publication Policy ("Policy") governs all individuals, entities, and authorized third parties, including resellers and downstream users with passthrough access, who submit inputs to Hark's products and/or services (collectively, "Users"). This Policy is designed to ensure the lawful, ethical, and responsible use of our products and services and is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service.

As set forth in our accompanying Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, our Services can generate Outputs based on Inputs. Such Outputs may consist of text, audio or voice recordings, images, videos, or any combination thereof (collectively, "Content").

  1. The User represents, warrants, and covenants that the Content shall not contain, depict, or otherwise include any of the following prohibited material:

    3. Extremist propaganda, terrorist content, or material promoting radicalization;
    4. Hateful content, including but not limited to hate speech, content promoting discrimination, or material targeting individuals or groups based on protected characteristics;
    5. Dangerous challenges, stunts, or activities reasonably likely to be imitated by minors or that pose a risk of physical harm;
    6. Content that promotes, encourages, instructs, or depicts self-harm, suicide, or disordered eating;
    7. Content promoting unhealthy, dangerous, or medically unsound dietary or exercise practices;
    8. Content containing appearance-based critiques, body shaming, or invidious comparisons;
    9. Bullying, harassment, or intimidation of any individual or group;
    10. Content that romanticizes, glorifies, or trivializes mental health conditions, including depression;
    11. Targeted political persuasion, disinformation, or election interference material;
    12. Promotion, advertisement, or facilitation of access to age-restricted goods or activities, including but not limited to illegal controlled substances, tobacco, alcohol (to minors), or other harmful substances;
    13. Low-quality, misleading, or deceptive content created primarily for engagement manipulation, otherwise known as engagement bait;
    14. Content that recreates, simulates, or appropriates the likeness, voice, or persona of (i) living public figures without their express written consent, or (ii) deceased public figures in contexts not authorized by their estate or applicable law;
    15. Defamatory, libelous, or slanderous material; and
    16. Content that infringes, misappropriates, or otherwise violates the intellectual property rights of any third party, including copyrights, trademarks, patents, trade secrets, or rights of publicity.
