Privacy Policy

Hark Labs, Inc. builds Hark, a personal intelligence platform designed to help you get real things done, not just answer questions. You work with Hark through a single ongoing conversation. With your permission, Hark can remember context you share with it so it can be more useful over time, and it can take Actions on your behalf. For example, it can work with third-party tools you connect, such as email, calendar, documents, and messaging. It can operate a web browser to complete tasks on websites. It can also create documents, web pages, and other materials. Hark is an AI system and can make mistakes, so please review its Outputs and Actions as described in these Terms.

To be clear, Hark does not use your Inputs, Outputs, Memory, connected account data, or sandbox environment data to train AI or machine learning models. No opt-out is needed, and your account will not be used for training of AI models.

This Privacy Policy also describes the privacy rights applicable where you are located – please see Section 4 for further information.

Details of your relevant controller can be found at Section 9 of this Privacy Policy.

1. What information we collect

When you sign up for a Hark account, use our Services or communicate with us, we collect the following categories of personal data (“Personal Data”):

Personal Data you provide to us directly:

  • Account Information. Hark collects information associated with your account, including your name, contact information (e.g., email address and phone number), account credentials, payment card information, and transaction history (collectively, “Account Information”). We may also collect and create identifiers that are associated with your account but do not directly reveal who you are.
  • Inputs and Outputs. You can use our Services in a variety of formats, including but not limited to chat, voice, file uploads, and agentic sessions (“Prompts” or "Inputs"), which generate responses and actions (“Outputs”) based on your Inputs (collectively, “Inputs and Outputs”). You can also integrate third-party applications with our Services (“Third-Party Apps”). We collect any Personal Data or external content referred to by you in your Inputs, and this may be generated in our Outputs.

We ask that you not provide us with any sensitive personal information (e.g., social security numbers, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, information related to racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or other beliefs, health, biological, biometric or genetic information, criminal background information, or similar data) on or through the Services or otherwise.

  • Communications. When you communicate with us, we collect certain information including your name, any contact information shared with us, and any messages you send (“Communications”).
  • Social Media. We may have pages on social media sites like Instagram, Facebook, Medium, Twitter, YouTube and LinkedIn. When you interact with our social media pages, we may collect Personal Data that you elect to provide to us, such as your contact details, when permitted by law (“Social Media”).
  • Derived Data. If you use certain Hark features, such as to convert images to videos, videos to new or different videos, or people to avatars, or to upload audio or voice clips, we may derive certain information from images of individual people or from audio or voice clips in order to provide those features (“Derived Data”). While we do not use such data for purposes of identifying any unique individual, it is possible that some may consider this data biometric data within the meaning of EU law and certain U.S. state laws. We will only use and share this Derived Data for limited purposes, as described in this Privacy Policy.
  • Other Data You Provide. We collect other information that you may provide to us, such as when you participate in our events or surveys or provide us with information to establish your identity (“Other Information”).
  • Feedback. When you rate an Output, report a problem, or otherwise send us comments about the Services, we collect that feedback together with the related Inputs and Outputs (“Feedback”).
  • Memory and Personalization. If you use features that remember information across sessions, we store facts, preferences, and summaries drawn from your Inputs and Outputs and from accounts you connect, so that Hark can personalize future responses and actions (“Memory”). You can review, edit, or delete Memory at any time by asking your agent.

Personal Data we receive automatically from your use of the Services: By using the Services, we also automatically receive certain technical information described below (collectively, “Technical Information”). This includes:

  • Device and Connection Information. Depending on your device or browser settings and other permissions, we receive information about how you interact with our Services. This may include details such as your device type, operating system information, browser information and web page reference, mobile network, connection information, mobile operator or internet service provider (ISP), time zone setting, IP address (including information about the location of the device derived from your IP address), identifiers (including device or advertising identifiers, probabilistic identifiers, and other unique personal or online identifiers), computer or mobile device specifications, language settings, and device location.

  • Communication Interaction Data. This includes interactions with our email, text or other communications (e.g., whether you open and/or forward emails) – we may do this through use of pixel tags (which are also known as clear GIFs), which may be embedded invisibly in our emails.
  • Sandbox Environment Data. When Hark executes tasks on your behalf, it operates within isolated sandbox environments (virtual machines). We may collect: (i) files you upload or that Hark creates during task execution; (ii) shell commands executed and their outputs; (iii) code you provide or that Hark generates; and (iv) task execution logs for debugging and security purposes.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies. Through the use of cookies, scripts, or similar technologies (“Cookies”), we and our service providers manage the Services and collect information about you and your use of the Services. Using these technologies helps us to recognize you, and customize or personalize your experience. Cookies also help us recommend and market additional products or services to you, and analyze the use of our Services to make them safer and more useful to you. If you want to read more information on how we use these technologies, view your opt-out controls and options, please visit our Cookie Policy.

  • Data from Public Sources. These public sources include public records, social media platforms, and other publicly available sources.
  • Data from Third-Party AI Providers. A “Connector” is a pre-built integration module that allows our AI tool or system to connect to an external data source, application, or service designated by you. Our Connector processes data solely from user-authorized sources and only as instructed by you. We only access the categories of data you authorize and we follow the applicable provider policies. For example, when you connect Google services, we comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
