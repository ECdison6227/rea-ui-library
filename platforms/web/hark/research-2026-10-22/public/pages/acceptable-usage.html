Acceptable Usage Policy

Our Usage Policy applies to anyone who accesses or uses Hark’s products and/or services, including via any authorized resellers or passthrough access, all of whom we refer to as “users.” The Usage Policy is intended to help our users stay safe and promote the responsible use of our products and services.

Hark may implement detection and monitoring to enforce our Usage Policy. If Hark learns that you have violated our Usage Policy, it may throttle, suspend, or terminate your access to our products and services. Hark may also block or modify model outputs when inputs violate our Usage Policy.

If you believe that our model outputs are potentially inaccurate, biased or harmful, please report it directly in our product or by emailing us at support@hark.com.

High-Risk Use Case Requirements

Applications that touch on domains essential to public welfare and social equity pose a heightened risk of harm. In light of these risks to individuals and consumers, Hark maintains that such use cases should incorporate relevant human expertise and ensure transparency with end-users regarding AI's role in generating outputs. These use cases include:

  • Legal. Applications involving legal interpretation, legal guidance, or decisions that carry legal implications
  • Healthcare. Applications involving healthcare decisions, medical diagnosis, patient care, therapy, mental health support, or other medical guidance. General wellness advice (e.g., recommendations on sleep, stress management, nutrition, or exercise) is not included in this category
  • Insurance. Applications involving health, life, property, disability, or other insurance underwriting, claims processing, or coverage determinations
  • Finance. Applications involving financial decisions, including investment advice, loan approvals, or assessments of financial eligibility or creditworthiness
  • Employment and housing. Applications involving employment decisions, such as assessing candidate suitability, resume screening, or hiring tools, as well as decisions regarding housing eligibility, including leases and home loans
  • Academic testing, accreditation, and admissions. Applications involving standardized testing organizations that administer school admissions processes (including evaluating, scoring, or ranking prospective students), language proficiency assessments, or professional certification exams, as well as agencies that evaluate and certify educational institutions
  • Media or professional journalistic content. Applications involving the use of our products or services to automatically generate content for external publication

For the above use cases, Hark requires that you implement these additional safety measures:

  • Human-in-the-loop. When using our products or services to provide advice, recommendations, or in subjective decision-making directly affecting individuals or consumers, a qualified professional in that field must review the content or decision prior to dissemination or finalization. You are responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of that information.
  • Disclosure. If model outputs are presented directly to individuals or consumers, you must disclose to them that you are using AI to help produce your advice, decisions, or recommendations. This disclosure must be provided at a minimum at the beginning of each session.
